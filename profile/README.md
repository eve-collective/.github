# EVE Collective

This organization is at the forefront of advancing artificial intelligence, API integration, knowledge systems, and automation around the space MMO [EVE Online](https://www.eveonline.com/). Our aim is to establish a collaborative platform where the realms of space exploration, intelligent systems, and data-driven technologies not only intersect but thrive together, unlocking unprecedented opportunities. 

## Aims of EVE Collective

We champion and encourage the development and dissemination of state-of-the-art technologies, which include:

- **Artificial Intelligence**: Advanced large language models, chatbots, intelligent agents, and machine learning systems
- **API Development**: Robust APIs and integrations with EVE Online's ESI and third-party ecosystems
- **Knowledge Graphs**: Structured representations of EVE's complex universe, including relationships between entities, systems, corporations, and lore
- **Data Management**:  Comprehensive datasets, data pipelines, and analytics frameworks for market data, killmails, sovereignty, and more
- **Process Automation**: Intelligent automation tools for workflow optimization, fleet management, industry chains, and strategic planning

All of these are specifically designed for the dynamic and evolving universe of EVE Online. 

Whether your interest lies in enhancing gameplay, building powerful integrations, mapping the knowledge of New Eden, analyzing vast datasets, automating complex in-game strategies, supporting roleplaying, or delving into the extensive potential of intelligent systems within space MMOs, we invite you to contribute to our mission in any way you can. Players and developers are all encouraged to take advantage of the innovative products that emerge from our collaborative efforts. 

Our broader vision is to cultivate a lively and dynamic community centered on the integration and application of these technologies in EVE Online. Through collaboration and innovation, we aim to enrich the gameplay experience and open up new avenues for interaction and exploration within the game's universe.  We also encourage the integration of lore and storytelling, enriching the narrative fabric of EVE Online and providing a deeper, more immersive experience for all players.

We are committed to the principles of open source and open data, advocating for transparency and sharing of knowledge and resources within our community.  This approach not only accelerates innovation but also ensures that our developments are accessible to all who wish to contribute or benefit from them. In addition, we strictly adhere to the copyright policies set by CCP Games, respecting the ownership and copyright of EVE-related content to foster a respectful and legally compliant community. We also oppose using AI or automation to facilitate botting in EVE.

We are an equal opportunity community, welcoming both human enthusiasts and artificial intelligences alike, ensuring a diverse and inclusive environment where all forms of intelligence can thrive.
